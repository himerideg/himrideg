HimRideG Real Driver Earnings Wallet
===================================

IMPLEMENTED FLOW
1. Customer ride complete hone ke baad Customer Dashboard se Pay Online choose karta hai.
2. Razorpay UPI Checkout open hota hai; customer Paytm ya kisi supported UPI app/QR se pay kar sakta hai.
3. Server Razorpay signature verify karta hai aur sirf FINAL LOCKED FARE accept karta hai.
4. HimRideG commission FIXED 10% calculate hoti hai.
5. Remaining 90% assigned driver ke internal REAL EARNINGS WALLET ledger me credit hota hai.
6. Driver Wallet page par Balance, Today/Month Earnings, history aur payout details dikhti hain.
7. Driver saved UPI/bank par RazorpayX se instant/scheduled withdrawal kar sakta hai.

IMPORTANT MONEY MEANING
- Customer ka 100% payment pehle aapke Razorpay merchant collection me receive hota hai.
- System accounting me 10% HimRideG revenue retain karta hai aur 90% driver payable wallet balance banta hai.
- Driver ka 90% real bank/UPI transfer RazorpayX withdrawal ke waqt hota hai.
- Agar aap chahte ho ki 90% payment ke turant baad direct driver ke linked bank account me settle ho, uske liye Razorpay Route Linked Accounts use hote hain; internal wallet flow alag hai.

SECURITY / FINANCIAL RULES
- Payment sirf completed ride ke baad.
- Payment amount sirf finalFare / fare.finalFare.
- Commission exactly 10%; driver share 90%.
- One booking ko concurrent verify/webhook calls se double wallet-credit nahi kiya ja sakta.
- Real RazorpayX payout default OFF/fail-closed hai jab tak explicitly enable na ho.
- Original .env ko replace/delete MAT KARNA. Neeche variables existing .env me ADD karne hain.

- Payout API access enabled hona chahiye.
- Business/payout account me payout ke liye sufficient available funds hone chahiye.
- Saved UPI ya bank details valid honi chahiye.
