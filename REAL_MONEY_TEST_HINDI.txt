F) REAL-MONEY SAFETY CHANGES
============================================================

- Browser callback alone par PAID nahi hota.
- Server signature verify + Razorpay payment fetch + amount/order/currency match karta hai.
- Authorized payment ko capture karta hai; captured ke bina driver wallet settle nahi hota.
- payment.captured webhook browser-close fallback hai.
- Payout request has mandatory X-Payout-Idempotency UUID.
- Timeout/5xx = outcome uncertain; internal wallet refund nahi hota until safely resolved.
- Scheduler SAME idempotency key se retry karta hai, duplicate payout se bachne ke liye.
- Payout webhook + scheduler dono state reconcile karte hain.
- payout reversed hua to wallet restore hota hai.
- Instant payout me RazorpayX low balance hua to direct failure + wallet restore.
